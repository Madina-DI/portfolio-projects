CONTEXT

From scattered operations to one understandable product flow.

The project was conceived as an internal CRM for construction work: users need different views, projects need clear status visibility, and routine actions should be structured rather than buried in messages or spreadsheets.

PRODUCT THINKING

Role-based flows instead of one generic dashboard.

USERSRole logic

Different screens and actions depending on responsibility.

PROJECTSStatus visibility

Operational state, workstreams and current actions in one system.

UXClear hierarchy

Information is grouped around decisions rather than raw data.

QAEnd-to-end tests

Playwright used to validate critical user flows.

BUILD

A product case, not a decorative dashboard.

The interface was developed in Next.js/React with attention to reusable components, route structure and realistic workflow behavior. The project is private, so this public case intentionally shows product logic rather than exposing business data.

WHAT IT PROVES

I can translate a business process into an interface.

This case connects my previous understanding of operational systems with product design and frontend implementation — useful for Creative Technologist, Product Builder and AI-enabled internal-tool roles.
