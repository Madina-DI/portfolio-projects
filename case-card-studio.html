Manual catalog work

Repeated data entry before every product launch

Product data often starts in a spreadsheet. Staff then re-enter facts, write descriptions and look for missing fields. Card Studio gathers this work in one interface so the reviewer can focus on facts and exceptions.

Preparation workflow

Import, draft and review before submission

Import XLSX or CSV, inspect each product, choose a WB category and load its characteristics. Optionally prepare AI titles and descriptions from the supplied facts. Check missing values, approve the data and submit a small batch.

Implemented in the prototype

Editable cards with explicit validation and status

The prototype includes Excel import/export, a product editor, category-characteristic checks and server routes for Claude and the WB Content API. Submission requires human approval. An accepted request and a card created in WB have separate states. API keys stay in the current tab and are not stored with catalog data.

Pilot and limits

A pilot on 10–20 products

The next step is to connect credentials and verify one real card in a seller account, then compare time per product and corrections across a small batch. No measured time savings or paid client deployments are claimed. One import row represents one article and size. Prices, inventory, variant merging, Ozon and 1C integrations are outside this version.
