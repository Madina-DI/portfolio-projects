THE PRODUCT

Turn an iPhone Pro into a practical room-measurement tool.

Obmer is being built for interior designers who need room dimensions, openings and geometry before design work begins. The app combines Apple's RoomPlan with raw ARKit LiDAR data instead of relying on a single simplified model.

MEASUREMENT SYSTEM

Automatic room capture plus manual LiDAR measurement.

ROOMPLANRoom structure

Captures walls, doors, windows and openings and converts dimensions to millimetres.

RAW MESHReal surface geometry

ARMeshAnchor data preserves beams, slopes, wall irregularities and geometry RoomPlan simplifies away.

DEPTH MAPManual two-point measure

A crosshair samples LiDAR depth directly so the user can measure blocked openings, beams or details automation misses.

TOP-UP SCANContinue the same session

Users can rescan missing areas without resetting the coordinate system; measurements are recalculated.

QUALITY CONTROL

The app does not pretend every scan is accurate.

Obmer checks scan completeness, suspicious wall counts, ceiling height, short fragments and open contours. Low-confidence measurements are flagged. Raw mesh is also used to detect ceiling drops and distinguish probable beams from incomplete or conflicting scan data.

EXPORT & WORKFLOW

Measurements leave the app in useful formats.

The current stage exports a parametric USDZ model, measurement JSON and raw OBJ mesh. This creates a foundation for later CAD/BIM workflows while keeping Stage 1 focused on reliable capture and verification.
