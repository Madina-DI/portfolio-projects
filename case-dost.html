THE CHALLENGE

Make an AI company feel technological without looking like another AI template.

The goal was to build a visual identity that behaves like a product experience. Instead of a static hero, the website needed a real-time scene that communicates services, motion and depth while remaining understandable to a business audience.

MY APPROACH

Use interaction as the brand language.

3D SYSTEM136 objects

Metallic cubes form a ribbon-like system; selected service cubes are interactive.

MATERIALSReal reflections

A cube camera feeds live environment reflections into the metallic surfaces.

ENERGYGLSL volume

Volumetric energy uses raymarching and procedural noise rather than pre-rendered video.

LOCALIZATION4 languages

Language choice changes page copy, labels and voice tracks together.

CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT

Designed for the browser, not exported into it.

The scene is rendered in real time with Three.js / WebGL. Performance logic pauses expensive rendering when the hero is outside the viewport. Content and color variables are structured so the site can be adjusted without rebuilding a full application stack.
