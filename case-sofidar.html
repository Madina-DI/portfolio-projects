THE PRODUCT

From isolated AI clips to a managed production pipeline.

Sofidar Studio is designed around the real workflow of AI-video production: brief, visual idea, storyboard, individual scenes, rendering, storage, credits and iteration. The goal is to make the production process controllable instead of relying on one-off generations.

MAIN FLOW

Brief → storyboard → scenes → render jobs.

The storyboard step can be generated through Claude or mocked locally for development without using a paid LLM key. Each scene is rendered as a separate job so a failed scene can be retried without losing already completed work.

SYSTEM DESIGN

Built as a product, not just a demo.

CREDITSLedger-based balance

Credit spending and refunds are recorded through an idempotent transaction ledger.

QUEUESubmit → poll → store

A worker sends jobs to the provider, polls status, downloads results and stores assets locally or in S3/R2.

REFERENCESAvatars and trend library

Avatar reference-image sets and trend analysis support repeatable visual production.

SECURITYAuth, roles and safe prompts

Google auth, roles, parameter validation and prompt-flag stripping reduce operational risk.

STATUS

Private product in active development.

The project has been renamed from Seedance Platform to Sofidar Studio to make the product brand independent from one provider. Seedance and Seedream remain model names inside the stack, while the platform architecture is designed so providers can be replaced or extended.
